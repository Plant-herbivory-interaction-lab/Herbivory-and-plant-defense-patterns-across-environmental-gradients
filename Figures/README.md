# fig_1.jpg
A principal component analysis (A) where PC1 (B: productivity) was explained by annual precipitation (C: AP), mean annual temperature (D: MAT), precipitation of the warmest quarter (E: PWQ), and yearly standard deviation of temperature (F: Tsd). The percentages in (A) indicate the variation explained on each PC axis. B) indicates a map of Solanum carolinense Inaturalist observations (small points) and observed field plant population locations (large points).
# SEM.jpg
The pSEMs of the most plausible models of the A) field and B) garden. Solid lines indicate a significant relationship between the predictor and the response, and the dashed lines indicate a non-significant relationship. Black lines indicate a positive relationship, and red lines indicate a negative relationship. Values indicate significant (p < 0.05) standardized estimates, and the widths of the lines indicate the strength of the significant relationships.
